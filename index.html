General

—
Budget period definition: Each labelled month covers 28th of the previous calendar month through 27th of that month. June 2026 = 28 May to 27 June 2026. June 2026 is the first allowed period.
Auto-rolls forward only when the current period falls outside your 12-month window. June 2026 minimum.
Applies from the active period onwards. Earlier months keep whatever they were budgeted at — changing this never rewrites history.
Modelling a pay rise? Don’t change this figure to test an idea. The scenario planner works out the rise after tax and National Insurance, shows what it does to your surplus, savings rate and pension, and only writes to the budget once you apply it.

🔑 Account & Security — Password

Password-gate the app on this device. This is a client-side gate — it protects against casual access but anyone with your gist token or a JSON backup can still read your data.

Two-factor authentication

—
A time-based code from an authenticator app, checked on top of your password. Everything is generated and verified in this browser — no phone number, no server, and it keeps working offline.

Data Management

Data lives in this browser's localStorage. Export a backup regularly.

Recommended: export a JSON backup weekly to your Backups/ folder.
